Privacy Policy

Inventory Tool is a private inventory and sales management tool used only by its owner for their own eBay seller account. It is not offered to other users.

With the owner's consent, the tool reads the owner's own eBay order and fee information through the eBay APIs to record sales and calculate profit. The data is stored in the owner's private database, is not sold or shared with any third party, and the access token is stored encrypted.

The owner can revoke access at any time from their eBay account settings.
